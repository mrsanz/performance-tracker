# 🚀 Phone Screen Prep Guide: Targeting Reddit

This guide outlines three critical areas to focus on for your phone screen, ensuring your preparation aligns with **Reddit's company culture** and the **specific role requirements**.

---

## 1. 🤝 Master Reddit's Company & Community Values

Reddit values authenticity, community, and impact. Be ready to subtly reference these concepts when describing your work style and experiences.

### Key Values to Highlight

| Category | Key Value | How to Weave it Into Your Answers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Community** | **Remember the Human** | Discuss empathy, respect, and ensuring a positive experience for users, customers, or internal teammates. |
| **Community** | **Start with Community** | Frame projects around empowering users or internal stakeholders. Show you prioritize *their* needs first. |
| **Company** | **Add Value** | Focus on the **results** and **impact** of your past work (e.g., "This project led to X% increase/decrease/saving"). |
| **Company** | **Evolve** | Highlight examples of learning new skills, adapting to a major change, or challenging the status quo to drive improvement. |
| **Company** | **Default Open** | Discuss clear, transparent communication and how you give and receive candid feedback. |

---

## 2. 🎯 Analyze the Role's DNA and Requirements

Identify the **top 3-5 core requirements** from the job description and make sure every story you tell addresses at least one of them.

### The Match-Up Exercise

1.  **Deconstruct the Job Description:** Highlight all **required technical skills**, **soft skills** (e.g., "highly collaborative," "bias for action"), and the **ultimate goal** of the role (e.g., "Improve system reliability").
2.  **Prepare S.T.A.R. Stories:** For each of the top requirements, prepare a concise story using the S.T.A.R. method:
    * **S**ituation: Set the scene briefly.
    * **T**ask: Clearly state the objective or challenge.
    * **A**ction: Describe **what *you* specifically did** (focus on your unique contribution).
    * **R**esult: **Quantify the outcome.** Always aim to include numbers, percentages, or measurable impacts.
3.  **Prepare Insightful Questions:** Your questions should show you've thought deeply about the role and the company's challenges.
    * *Good Example:* "Given Reddit's focus on 'Evolve,' what is the primary challenge this team is currently facing, and how would this role specifically contribute to overcoming it?"

---

## 3. ✨ Define Your Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

Don't just meet the requirements—show them why you are a **unique and essential** addition to their team.

* **Key Strengths:** Select strengths that align with both the **role** *and* Reddit's values.
    * *Example:* "My biggest strength is **Radical Prioritization**; I'm a self-starter who ensures our team is always focused on activities that truly 'Add Value' to the company's mission."
* **Unique Passions/Talents:** Mention relevant personal interests or background elements that connect you to the platform or the culture.
    * *Example:* "As a long-time moderator on the [Specific Subreddit], I have firsthand experience with **community dynamics** and the challenge of scaling the 'Remember the Human' value."
* **The Bridge/The Fixer:** Clearly articulate the unique problem your experience solves for them.
    * *Example:* "My background uniquely allows me to **bridge the gap** between our technical engineering goals and non-technical business strategy requirements, ensuring seamless communication."
